# AdOS — Executive Presentation Storyboard

**Companion to:** `PRESENTATION_CONSTITUTION.md`. This storyboard defines the
official ~20-slide executive deck. Documentation only — no slides are built here.
Every slide serves one idea and traces to the constitution's slide flow (§4) and
narrative arc (§5). The deck must be understandable by a non-technical board.

**Legend for each slide:** Objective · Title · Key Message · Visual · Diagram ·
Icons · Charts · Animation · Speaker Notes · Transition · Duration · Business
Impact · Customer Takeaway · Executive Takeaway · Questions answered.

**Global timing target:** 20 slides × ~60–90s ≈ 20–28 minutes + demo, leaving
room for discussion. Slides marked *(cuttable)* can be dropped if time is short.

---

## ACT I — The world changed

### Slide 1 — Title
- **Objective:** Set a premium, calm, trustworthy tone.
- **Title:** AdOS — The Enterprise AI Operating System.
- **Key Message:** Enterprise AI that never leaves your building.
- **Visual:** Full-bleed dark canvas, centered AdOS mark + wordmark, one-line
  positioning beneath.
- **Diagram:** None.
- **Icons:** Brand mark only.
- **Charts:** None.
- **Animation:** Logo fades in; the perimeter line draws once beneath the
  wordmark (the deck's signature motif, previewed).
- **Speaker Notes:** "Thank you for your time. In the next twenty minutes I'll
  show you how to use AI for real business results — without your data ever
  leaving your own systems."
- **Transition:** "Let's start with what's changed."
- **Duration:** 30s.
- **Business Impact:** Frames AdOS as infrastructure, not a gadget.
- **Customer Takeaway:** This is serious and enterprise-grade.
- **Executive Takeaway:** Worth my attention.
- **Questions answered:** What is this? (identity)

### Slide 2 — The shift
- **Objective:** Establish that AI adoption is now inevitable.
- **Title:** AI is no longer optional.
- **Key Message:** Every competitor is adopting AI; standing still is falling
  behind.
- **Visual:** A single bold statement on dark; a subtle upward motion line.
- **Diagram:** None.
- **Icons:** One trend/arrow icon.
- **Charts:** Optional single illustrative trend line (labeled "illustrative").
- **Animation:** Headline fades in; the line rises on click.
- **Speaker Notes:** "AI has moved from experiment to expectation. The question
  is no longer whether to adopt it — it's how to do so safely."
- **Transition:** "But there's a catch most vendors won't mention."
- **Duration:** 60s.
- **Business Impact:** Creates urgency without hype.
- **Customer Takeaway:** We must act.
- **Executive Takeaway:** Inaction is itself a risk.
- **Questions answered:** Why now?

### Slide 3 — The catch
- **Objective:** Name the hidden cost of mainstream AI.
- **Title:** Most AI means handing over your data.
- **Key Message:** Cloud AI sends your information to someone else's servers.
- **Visual:** A stylized data flow leaving a building into a distant cloud.
- **Diagram:** Simple: [Your data] → [Outside cloud]. The arrow is the problem.
- **Icons:** Building, cloud, outbound arrow.
- **Charts:** None.
- **Animation:** The data packet animates *out* of the building — the
  uncomfortable moment.
- **Speaker Notes:** "Almost every AI tool works by sending your data to an
  outside cloud and a third-party model. For many of you, that alone ends the
  conversation."
- **Transition:** "So what does that actually put at risk?"
- **Duration:** 75s.
- **Business Impact:** Surfaces the core objection early and owns it.
- **Customer Takeaway:** That's exactly my concern.
- **Executive Takeaway:** This vendor understands my constraint.
- **Questions answered:** What's the risk with normal AI?

### Slide 4 — The stakes
- **Objective:** Translate the risk into four business consequences.
- **Title:** Four risks you can't accept.
- **Key Message:** Loss of control, unpredictable cost, compliance exposure,
  vendor dependency.
- **Visual:** Four quadrants, one word each, minimal.
- **Diagram:** 2×2 concept grid (not data).
- **Icons:** Lock (control), coin (cost), shield-check (compliance), chain
  (dependency).
- **Charts:** None.
- **Animation:** Quadrants reveal one per click as the speaker names each.
- **Speaker Notes:** "Four things are at stake: control of your data, the cost of
  per-use AI fees, your compliance obligations under KVKK and GDPR, and
  dependence on a foreign provider's terms."
- **Transition:** "Now imagine turning this around."
- **Duration:** 90s.
- **Business Impact:** Gives each executive their own reason to care.
- **Customer Takeaway:** All four apply to us.
- **Executive Takeaway:** These are board-level risks.
- **Questions answered:** What business problems are we solving?

### Slide 5 — The reframe
- **Objective:** Pivot from problem to a new possibility.
- **Title:** What if the AI came to your data?
- **Key Message:** Keep the data; bring the intelligence to it.
- **Visual:** The outbound arrow from slide 3 reverses direction — intelligence
  flows *in*, nothing flows out.
- **Diagram:** [Local model] → inside [Your building], perimeter intact.
- **Icons:** Building, inbound node.
- **Charts:** None.
- **Animation:** The earlier outbound arrow visibly reverses — the pivot moment.
- **Speaker Notes:** "The whole problem disappears if we flip it: instead of
  sending your data to the AI, we bring the AI to your data."
- **Transition:** "That is exactly what AdOS is."
- **Duration:** 60s.
- **Business Impact:** Reframes the category on our terms.
- **Customer Takeaway:** That would solve it.
- **Executive Takeaway:** Elegant and safe.
- **Questions answered:** Is there a safe way to use AI?

## Storyboard guardrails
- One idea per slide; if two appear, split.
- Every slide answers "so what does this mean for us?"
- The perimeter/sovereignty spine recurs at slides 1, 5, 9, 20.
- The demo (14) is the peak and must show local, offline operation.
- No fabricated numbers; illustrative charts are labeled.
- Bilingual TR/EN parity for titles, key messages, and speaker notes (authored in
  `PRESENTATION_CONTENT.md`).
