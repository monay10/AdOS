## Delivery notes (whole deck)
- **Pace:** ~60–90 seconds per slide; the demo (in the storyboard as slide 14)
  is inserted after Security/Workflow when presenting live.
- **Language:** deliver fully in Turkish for OIZ and public-institution audiences;
  English or bilingual for international enterprise.
