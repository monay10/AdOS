### 1.2 Recitals

1. **Product.** AdOS licenses the **Enterprise AI Operating System for Advertising**
   (the "**Product**", defined in §2), a self-hosted, offline-capable, 100% local-AI
   software platform that takes a client's advertising objective through a
   **human-approved pipeline** (marketing brief → creative ad copy → campaign
   **draft** → performance report → executive dashboard). The Product **drafts**
   advertising campaigns for human approval; **it does not launch, publish, run, or

2. **Deployment model.** The Product is **customer self-hosted** on the customer's
   own infrastructure (on-premise, private cloud, or air-gapped). There is no vendor
   cloud service, no per-token or usage-metered billing, and no standing vendor

   partner of the type and tier set out in §3, to refer, resell, and/or implement
   the Product, and Vendor is willing to appoint Partner on the terms of this
   Agreement.

4. **Governing program.** This Agreement operates within, and is subordinate to, the
   AdOS Partner Program as described in the Partner Program Constitution.

## 3. Scope of Appointment

3.1 **Appointment.** Subject to this Agreement, Vendor appoints Partner, and Partner
accepts appointment, as a **`[Referral / Reseller / Implementation (Delivery)]`
Partner** at the **`[Registered / Silver / Gold / Platinum]`** Tier within the
**Territory** (Appendix A).

3.2 **Non-exclusive.** The appointment is **non-exclusive** and **non-transferable**.
Nothing in this Agreement grants Partner any exclusive right to any territory,
market, vertical, or Customer. Vendor may appoint other partners and may deal
directly with any Customer or prospect.

3.3 **Partner types.** The rights and duties of each partner type are:
- **Referral Partner** — introduces qualified leads; earns a referral fee
  (Appendix B). Does not contract for licenses.
- **Reseller Partner** — purchases licenses/subscriptions at a Tier discount and
  resells them to Customers under an End-User License.
- **Implementation (Delivery) Partner** — delivers installation, configuration,
  migration, training, and Tier-1 support on the Customer's infrastructure and
  **retains 100% of its own services revenue** (§8).

3.4 **(Roadmap) Technology / ISV Partner.** Appointment as a Technology/ISV Partner
building integrations is a **Roadmap** partner track that is **non-binding** and
gated on connector APIs that are themselves Roadmap (Appendix E). It confers no
present rights under this Agreement.

3.5 **No agency.** Partner is an independent contractor. This Agreement does not
create a partnership, joint venture, franchise, employment, or agency relationship.
Partner has no authority to bind Vendor, to make representations on Vendor's behalf
beyond the Documentation and Vendor's published materials, or to modify any Vendor
warranty.

3.6 **Tier changes.** Partner Tier is earned and reviewed per the criteria in
Appendix B and the Partner Program Constitution. Tier status may rise or fall on
review; Tier is not a contractual guarantee of any commercial outcome.

## 4. Responsibilities of the Parties

### 4.1 Partner responsibilities

Partner shall:
1. market and represent the Product **truthfully and consistently** with
   [../PRODUCT_TRUTH.md](../PRODUCT_TRUTH.md) and Vendor's published Documentation,
   and **not** state, warrant, or imply any capability the Product does not have
   (see §9.3 and Appendix E);
2. maintain the certified-staff headcount and other requirements for its Tier
   (Appendix B), referencing individual Certifications per
   [../customer-success/CERTIFICATION_PROGRAM.md](../customer-success/CERTIFICATION_PROGRAM.md);
3. for Implementation Partners, deliver installation and configuration in accordance
   with [../INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md](../INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md) and
   [../DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md), and train per
   [../customer-success/ADMINISTRATOR_TRAINING.md](../customer-success/ADMINISTRATOR_TRAINING.md)
   and [../customer-success/END_USER_TRAINING.md](../customer-success/END_USER_TRAINING.md);
4. provide **Tier-1** Customer support and escalate per §14 and
   [../customer-success/SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md](../customer-success/SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md);
5. register qualified opportunities via Deal Registration (Appendix B), keep
   pipeline and forecast information current, and participate in the review cadence
   (§4.3);
6. comply with all applicable laws and with §12 (Compliance);
7. **not** collect, transmit, or rely on any vendor telemetry from Customer
   instances (there is none) — all Partner-reported metrics (pipeline, forecasts,
   CSAT, Customer health) are **Partner-reported or Customer-shared**, not
   auto-collected;
8. protect Vendor Confidential Information (§7) and use the Marks only as permitted
   (§5).

### 4.2 Vendor responsibilities

Vendor shall:
1. make the Product and Documentation available to Partner for authorized resale,
   referral, and/or implementation activity;
2. provide reasonable enablement and access to the partner certification path;
3. honor accepted Deal Registrations for the applicable protection window
   (Appendix B);
4. provide **Tier-2 and product-defect** escalation support per §14, subject to the
   response targets in Appendix C (response targets only — Vendor has **no standing
   access** to Customer instances and does not perform remote fixes without
   Customer-granted access);
5. pay or credit referral fees and apply Tier discounts as set out in Appendix B and
   §8;
6. give notice of material changes to the Program per §16.

### 4.3 Joint cadence

The Parties shall follow the Program cadence: Deal Registration on each opportunity →
monthly pipeline sync → **quarterly business review (QBR)** → annual Tier review and
renewal (§15).

## 8. Revenue & Payment

8.1 **Legitimate revenue streams.** Partner revenue under the Program consists solely
of:
1. **License/subscription resale margin** — the difference between the Tier discount
   price at which a Reseller Partner purchases and the price at which it resells an
   End-User License (Appendix B);
2. **Implementation & services revenue** — fees Partner charges Customers for
   delivery services (installation, configuration, migration, training, managed
   services), **100% retained by Partner**;
3. **Support / managed-services revenue** — fees Partner charges for Tier-1 and
   ongoing operational support it provides (§14);
4. **Referral fees** — fees Vendor pays a Referral Partner for a qualified,
   converted referral (Appendix B).

8.2 **No usage or cloud metering.** The Product has **no** per-token, per-seat
metered, consumption, or hosted-inference billing, and there is no vendor cloud
markup. Partner shall not describe, invoice, or market any consumption- or
cloud-usage-based charge as an AdOS charge. Licensing is a contractual grant, not a
metered entitlement.

8.3 **Discounts & fees.** Tier discounts, referral rates, and Deal Registration
protection windows are set out in **Appendix B** and are **illustrative** unless
fixed in a signed order or Program schedule.

8.4 **Invoicing & payment.** For resale, Partner is invoiced at the Tier discount
price and pays within `[30]` days of invoice. Referral fees are payable within
`[45]` days after Vendor's receipt of the corresponding Customer payment. Amounts are
in `[Currency]` and are exclusive of taxes.

8.5 **Taxes.** Each Party is responsible for its own taxes; amounts payable are
exclusive of, and Partner is responsible for, applicable sales, use, VAT, or similar
taxes other than taxes on Vendor's net income.

8.6 **Records & audit.** Partner shall keep accurate records of resale and referral
activity and, on reasonable notice, allow Vendor to audit them once per `[12]`-month
period solely to verify amounts due.

8.7 **No standing metrics collection.** Because there is no vendor telemetry, all
revenue, pipeline, and performance figures are **Partner-reported or
Customer-shared**; Vendor relies on Partner's records under §8.6, not on data
collected from Customer instances.

## 16. Support Obligations

16.1 **Self-hosted support model.** Because the Product is customer self-hosted, the
Implementation/Delivery Partner provides **Tier-1** support and is the Customer's
first line of contact. Vendor provides **Tier-2 and product-defect** escalation.
Vendor SLAs are **response** targets only; Vendor has **no standing access** to
Customer instances and performs remote work only with Customer-granted access.

16.2 **Severity model (Sev 1–4).** The Parties use the severity classification in
[../customer-success/SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md](../customer-success/SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md):

| Severity | Description (summary) | Partner (Tier-1) | Vendor (Tier-2/defect) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sev 1** | Critical — production down / no workaround | Triage, engage Customer, escalate immediately | Response per Appendix C |
| **Sev 2** | High — major function impaired, workaround exists | Triage, workaround, escalate | Response per Appendix C |
| **Sev 3** | Medium — minor/partial impact | Resolve or escalate | Response per Appendix C |
| **Sev 4** | Low — question, cosmetic, enhancement | Resolve at Tier-1 | Best-effort / Roadmap intake |

16.3 **Escalation.** Partner shall triage, attempt Tier-1 resolution, and escalate
unresolved Sev 1/Sev 2 issues to Vendor promptly with reproduction detail, referencing
[../customer-success/SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md](../customer-success/SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md) and,
for recovery scenarios, [../DISASTER_RECOVERY.md](../DISASTER_RECOVERY.md).

16.4 **Managed services.** Partner may offer paid managed-services support (§8.1(3))
under its own terms, provided such offerings do not misrepresent the Product (§9.3).
