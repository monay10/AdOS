# AdOS — User Guide

AdOS runs your advertising like an autonomous agency. You state a goal; the AI
company plans and executes it, pausing for your approval at each stage.

## Sign in

Go to your AdOS URL and sign in with your work email and **company name**. Your
company is your private, isolated workspace — nobody else sees your data. The
interface appears in **Turkish or English automatically**, matching your
browser/computer language.

## The flow

AdOS guides you through a short setup, then runs Missions.

1. **Workspace** — your company's home for everything below.
2. **Client** — the customer you're marketing for.
3. **Brand** — voice, values and rules the AI must respect.
4. **Product** — what the client sells.
5. **Project** *(optional)* — groups a brand's work.
6. **Mission** — a business objective in plain words (e.g. *"Acquire new
   patients for a dental clinic opening next month"*), with a budget and a
   target metric.

## Running a Mission

Open a Mission and move through the pipeline. **Each stage waits for your
approval before the next unlocks:**

1. **Marketing Brief** — strategy: objective, audience, positioning, channels,
   budget split, KPIs. → *Approve*.
2. **Creative** — headline, ad copy, CTA, social post, landing page, email. →
   *Approve*.
3. **Campaign** — channels, budgets, ad sets, schedule. → *Approve*.
4. **Analytics** — enter real results; AdOS computes KPIs (ROAS, ROI, CTR) with
   an AI summary and recommendations.
5. **CEO Dashboard** — an executive verdict, key results, decisions, next actions.
6. **Learning** — records the outcome so the company gets smarter over time.

You can **cancel** a Mission at any point (with an optional reason); it's audited
and the tenant stays healthy.

## Other tools

- **Approvals** — route any decision through Draft → In Review → Approved /
  Rejected / Revision Requested, with a full timeline.
- **Assets** — a versioned library of images, copy, documents and links.
- **Reports** — a saved snapshot of a client's performance to share with them.
- **Lists** — Briefs, Creative, Campaigns, Analytics, Executive dashboards.

## Tips

- The **activity feed** on the dashboard shows every event as it happens.
- Approvals are yours — nothing publishes or advances without you.
- Everything runs on your own systems; no data leaves your infrastructure.
