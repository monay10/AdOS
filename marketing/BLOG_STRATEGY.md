# AdOS Blog Strategy — First 100 Articles

**Owner:** Office of the Chief Marketing Officer (Content)
**Status:** Official — binding on all blog content
**Aligned to:** `PRODUCT_TRUTH.md` (source of truth), `CANON.md`,
`marketing/MARKETING_CONSTITUTION.md` (v1.0.0)

**Product:** AdOS — **Enterprise AI Operating System for Advertising**. An
offline-first, 100% local-AI platform that takes a client's advertising objective
through a **human-approved pipeline** (marketing brief → creative copy → campaign
draft → performance report → executive dashboard) and remembers what works in a
marketing-performance **Company Brain**. It **drafts** campaigns; it does not launch
live ads.

---

## How this plan maps to the funnel and topic clusters

This plan seeds the **Awareness (TOFU)** engine defined in Constitution §11–§12:
evergreen, teacherly content that makes the buyer *want* sovereignty before we ask
for a demo. It is organized into 17 topic clusters (the category headings below),
each a self-reinforcing hub of related posts that build topical authority around
the questions we intend to own: *sovereign advertising AI, on-prem AI, local LLM,
marketing-performance memory, şirket içi yapay zeka, yapay zeka veri güvenliği*.

**Funnel mapping (each post declares one primary CTA per §20):**

- **Awareness / TOFU (majority):** category-education and problem-framing posts.
  CTA **"Read the Guide"** (TR *"Rehberi Okuyun"*) or **"Talk to Sales"** (TR
  *"Satışla Görüşün"*).
- **Consideration / MOFU:** posts that explain the AdOS mechanism (Company Brain
  marketing-performance memory, the AI-assisted campaign pipeline, approval gates)
  or invite ROI thinking. CTA **"See the Platform"** (TR *"Platformu Keşfedin"*) or
  **"Calculate Your ROI"** (TR *"ROI'nizi Hesaplayın"*).
- **Decision / BOFU:** vertical proof and buyer-committee pieces closest to a
  purchase. CTA **"Request a Demo"** (TR *"Demo Talep Edin"*).

**Cluster architecture.** Foundational clusters (Artificial Intelligence, On-Prem
AI, Local LLM, Cyber Security) frame the *problem* and the *category*. Product
clusters (Company Brain, AI Campaign Pipeline, Campaign Approval Pipeline, Marketing
Performance Memory) explain the *mechanism*. Strategy clusters (Digital
Transformation, Enterprise Architecture) speak to the buying committee's agenda.
Vertical clusters (Manufacturing, OSB, Government, Healthcare, Retail, Education,
Finance) carry the same claims into each ICP segment (§10) — reframed to each
segment's advertising and campaign work — and pull traffic toward MOFU/BOFU.
Internal links flow child → hub → platform/feature page → demo.

**Bilingual rule (§18):** every post ships TR + EN with identical claims and
numbers; TR is native, not literal. Product names (AdOS, Company Brain) stay
English in both. Turkish titles are provided as a starting draft.

**Voice guardrail:** no banned words (§4); benefit-before-mechanism; honest about
the local-inference performance trade-off; honest that AdOS **drafts** campaigns and
never launches live ads; every claim traces to CANON / PRODUCT_TRUTH.
