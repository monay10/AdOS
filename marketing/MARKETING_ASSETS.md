## Purpose

This catalog defines **every marketing asset AdOS produces**, its intended job,
its exact dimensions, format, owning role, and delivery priority. It is the
production backlog for the brand and creative team and the acceptance list for
agencies.

Every asset conforms to the Marketing Constitution — voice (§4), messaging
(§6), and especially **visual standards (§19)**:

- **Dark-first, sovereign, calm** — deep near-black canvas, a single confident
  accent, high-contrast text meeting WCAG AA. Light theme mirrors it.
- **Imagery is data-sovereignty metaphor** — walls, buildings, vaults, grounded
  architecture — **never** stock "robot / glowing brain" AI clichés.
- **Diagrams show data staying inside the boundary** — on-prem architecture,
  vector, editable, theme-aware; the boundary line is the hero.
- **No external copyrighted assets.** Everything is owned or licensed; original
  vector illustration is preferred. No stock photos of servers we do not own.
- **Bilingual TR/EN parity** — every buyer-facing asset ships in both languages
  with identical claims and numbers; product names (AdOS, Company Brain,
  Mission) stay in English in both.
- **Every asset carries exactly one primary CTA** from the canonical list (§20)
  and declares its funnel stage (§11).

**Owner roles referenced below:** Brand Designer, Motion Designer, Web Designer,
Content Designer, Product Marketing Manager (PMM), Demand Gen, Sales Enablement,
Video Producer.

**Priority scale:** **P0** = required for launch / core funnel; **P1** =
important, ships within one quarter of launch; **P2** = nice-to-have, backlog.

  screenshots, air-gap/multi-tenant/deploy diagrams, ROI and campaign-pipeline

  architecture explainers, hero loop and lower-thirds.

  covers, wide/TR roll-up variants, floor decals and table throws, executive and
  digital business cards, campaign/event signature banners, vertical-specific
  infographics and demo cuts, decorative micro-animations and loaders.
