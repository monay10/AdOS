## 0. Purpose

This Constitution governs all AdOS marketing. Every marketing artifact —
website content, SEO plan, blog, LinkedIn engine, press kit, launch campaign,
and asset catalog — **must** conform to the brand, voice, messaging, and claims
defined here. It is the marketing counterpart to `sales/SALES_KIT_CONSTITUTION.md`
and inherits its canonical facts verbatim (§1). Where any marketing document
contradicts this Constitution, this document wins.

The mandate: **build demand for AdOS without ever compromising the truth of what
it is** — a sovereign, on-premise, enterprise AI operating system for advertising.

---

## 1. Inherited canonical facts (binding — do not restate differently)

> **Alignment note (2026-07-27):** re-grounded against a source-of-code audit
> (`../PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`). Only implemented capabilities are stated as facts;
> roadmap items are labeled.

AdOS is an **Enterprise AI Operating System for advertising** — an offline-first
**AI advertising-agency platform** that runs **entirely on the customer's own
infrastructure**. A client states a marketing objective (a **Mission**) and AdOS
drafts the work through a human-approved pipeline: **Marketing Brief → Creative Set
→ Campaign Draft → Performance Report → Executive Dashboard**, on the agency model
Workspaces → Clients → Brands → Products → Missions. **Company Brain** is AdOS's
**marketing-performance memory** (brand/creative/campaign insight, a
campaign→lead→ROI knowledge graph, winning-ad patterns) — not a document Q&A system
and not a source of citations.

Non-negotiable truths: **Local AI** (runs on the customer's hardware via Ollama or
any OpenAI-compatible local engine — no cloud, no external API, no keys, no
internet required) · **data sovereignty** (customer data never leaves the
premises) · **on-premise** · **offline/air-gap capable** (default AI mode is
deterministic offline; connect a local engine for live output) · **tenant
isolation** (application-level; DB-level RLS is roadmap) · **human-gated** (every
step needs explicit approval; nothing auto-launches) · **bilingual TR/EN** · **no
per-token billing** · **no vendor lock-in** · honest performance trade-off (local
CPU inference is seconds, not milliseconds; better hardware closes the gap).

Not yet implemented (do not claim as shipped): document knowledge base / cited
answers, autonomous "Digital Employees," enforced per-user permission-aware AI,
immutable audit trail, external connectors, and live ad-platform launch. See
`../POSITIONING_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`.

**The one sentence:** *"AdOS is an Enterprise AI Operating System for advertising
that runs 100% on your own infrastructure — it drafts your briefs, creative, and
campaigns with local AI, your data never leaves your building, and it works with no
internet at all."*

These facts are shared with the Sales Kit. Any change propagates to both.

## 2. Brand strategy

- **Category:** We define and own **"Sovereign Enterprise AI"** — AI that lives
  entirely inside the customer's walls. We are not competing to be the best cloud
  assistant; we are creating the on-premise alternative to it.
- **Strategic wedge:** the growing tension between wanting AI and refusing to send
  data to someone else's cloud. AdOS resolves it.
- **Proof over promise:** the brand earns trust by showing (a drafted campaign,
  air-gap demos, the approval timeline and activity log), not by adjectives.
- **Turkey-first, sovereignty-universal:** native TR/EN; built for markets where
  data residency is a mandate, not a preference.

---

## 3. Brand personality

Five traits (use them to sanity-check any creative):

1. **Sovereign** — self-reliant, in control, independent.
2. **Grounded** — evidence-based, grounded in campaign performance, honest about trade-offs.
3. **Precise** — engineering-grade accuracy; no vague hype.
4. **Composed** — calm authority, not loud disruption.
5. **Respectful** — treats the buyer as an expert; never condescends.

Personality archetype: **the Sage/Ruler** — trusted expert who hands the customer
control, not a flashy Magician promising miracles.

---

## 4. Brand voice

- Confident, precise, honest, executive-readable.
- Verbs and numbers over adjectives. Short sentences.
- Explains, never hypes. Concedes honest trade-offs to build trust.
- Speaks to a smart, skeptical buyer (CIO/CISO/CEO), not a consumer.
- Product terms stay in English in both languages: **AdOS**, **Company Brain**,
  **Mission**.

**Banned words:** revolutionary, magic(al), game-changer, disrupt, cutting-edge,
next-gen, world-class, seamless(ly) (as filler), unleash, supercharge, 10x (as a
vague claim), "AI-powered" as a standalone boast.

**Preferred register:** sovereign, private, on your infrastructure, offline,
grounded, human-approved, drafts, on your terms, no data leaves your building.

## 6. Messaging framework

Every message follows **Sovereignty → Capability → Outcome** (shared with Sales §8):

- **Sovereignty (differentiator):** your data and your AI stay under your control,
  on your hardware, offline-capable.
- **Capability (mechanism):** Company Brain, the human-approved campaign pipeline,
  Workflows & Approvals — local, deterministic, human-gated.
- **Outcome (payoff):** faster campaign drafts, fewer stalled approvals, retained
  marketing know-how (winning-ad patterns), lower agency cost, quantifiable ROAS/ROI.

**Three headline value pillars (always this order):** **Sovereign · Capable ·
Accountable.**

**Message hierarchy:**
- **Primary:** "Enterprise AI that never leaves your building."
- **Secondary:** "A real AI operating system for advertising — Company Brain, a
  human-approved campaign pipeline, Workflows — not a chatbot."
- **Tertiary:** "Grounded in your campaign performance, human-approved at every stage."

## 12. Awareness strategy

- **Own the question, not just the keyword:** rank for "on-prem AI", "local LLM
  enterprise", "data sovereignty AI", "yapay zeka veri güvenliği", "şirket içi
  yapay zeka".
- **Educate first:** evergreen blog clusters (On-Prem AI, Local LLM, Company
  Brain, Marketing Performance, Security) that teach the buyer to want sovereignty.
- **Founder & expert POV** on LinkedIn: sharp, contrarian-but-true takes on
  cloud-AI data risk.
- **PR** around the launch and the category ("Sovereign Enterprise AI").
- **Bilingual from day one** — every awareness asset ships TR/EN.

---

## 13. Consideration strategy

- Deep **platform and feature pages** that pair benefit → mechanism → proof.
- **Case studies** (clearly illustrative) per vertical.
- **Webinars / demo sessions** on NovaMak: "watch it draft a campaign; watch it
  work air-gapped."
- **Comparison content** by category (cloud vs local; point tool vs operating
  system; build vs buy) — never name-and-shame.
- **ROI calculator** as an interactive consideration tool.

---

## 14. Decision strategy

- **Live demo** to discovered pain (NovaMak), with the five mandatory proof
  moments (a drafted marketing brief, an assembled campaign draft, Company Brain
  surfacing winning-ad patterns, a human approval gate, pull-the-cable/air-gap).
- **Security page + architecture page** to satisfy CISO/CTO.
- **ROI model** built with the buyer; **proposal** with objective acceptance
  criteria.
- Remove risk: pilot on the customer's own hardware.

---

## 15. Retention strategy

- Onboarding content, admin/operator guides, and in-product help that drive
  successful first value (seed the Company Brain, run the first Mission to a
  campaign draft).
- Quarterly value reviews tied to the ROI model's outcomes.
- Roadmap transparency; upgrade/backup/DR runbooks.
- Health metric: time-to-first-campaign-draft and adoption rate.

---

## 16. Advocacy strategy

- Convert successful customers into references, quotes, and (with consent) named
  case studies — replacing today's illustrative ones only when real and approved.
- Community around sovereign AI practitioners (IT/BT, security leads).
- Partner/channel co-marketing (system integrators, OSB bodies, public-sector).
- Advocacy metric: reference customers created, inbound referrals.

## 17. Campaign naming rules

- **Pattern:** `AdOS · <Theme> · <Year>[/Quarter]` — e.g. `AdOS · Sovereign AI ·
  2026`, `AdOS · Company Brain · 2026Q3`.
- Themes come from the message hierarchy (§6) or a pillar (Sovereign/Capable/
  Accountable) or a vertical.
- Bilingual campaign names keep the AdOS prefix and English theme token; the
  descriptive tagline is localized.
- No internal codenames in public-facing material. No hype words (§4) in names.
- UTM convention: `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign=ados_<theme>_<yyyy>`.

---

## 18. Writing standards

- Follow the voice (§4) and banned-word list. Active voice. Sentences ≤ 25 words
  where possible. One idea per paragraph.
- **Benefit before mechanism:** state the outcome, then how AdOS delivers it.
- Every factual claim traces to §1 or a clearly labeled model/illustrative source.
- Numbers are specific and honest; ranges are labeled as estimates.
- Bilingual parity: no claim or number exists in only one language. TR is native,
  not literal. Product names stay English.
- Accessibility: plain language, expand acronyms on first use (OSB, DR, RBAC).
- Every page/post ends with exactly one primary CTA (§20).

## 19. Visual standards

*(Detailed tokens live in the website design system; this sets the rules.)*

- **Palette:** dark-first, sovereign and calm — deep near-black canvas, a single
  confident accent, high-contrast text. Light theme mirrors it. Meet WCAG AA
  contrast.
- **Typography:** one clean, modern sans for UI/headers; readable body; generous
  spacing. No decorative display faces.
- **Imagery:** abstract/architectural, data-sovereignty metaphors (walls,
  buildings, grounded structures) — **not** stocky "robot/brain" AI clichés.
- **Diagrams:** precise, on-prem architecture; show data staying inside the
  boundary. Vector, editable, theme-aware.
- **Logo:** protect clear space; never recolor, distort, add effects, or place on
  low-contrast backgrounds (rules detailed in the Press Kit).
- **No external copyrighted assets.** Own or license everything; prefer original
  vector illustration.

## 21. Success metrics

**Awareness:** organic sessions, keyword rankings (TR/EN), impressions/reach,
share of voice on "sovereign/on-prem AI."
**Consideration:** qualified traffic, time-on-page for platform/feature pages,
ROI-calculator completions, webinar/demo registrations.
**Decision:** demo requests (MQL→SQL), proposal requests, pipeline influenced.
**Retention/Advocacy:** adoption content engagement, references created, referrals.
**Content health:** publish cadence hit, bilingual parity rate, Core Web Vitals
pass rate, % pages with valid structured data.

North-star for marketing: **qualified demo requests** from ICP accounts.

## 22. Marketing governance

- The CMO office owns this Constitution and the brand.
- **Single source of truth:** canonical facts (§1) and terminology are shared with
  the Sales Kit; any change updates both, in the same change.
- **Review gate:** every public artifact passes a brand+fact+bilingual check
  before publish; the marketing package is "official" only when
  `MARKETING_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` passes with zero contradictions.
- **Claim discipline:** no invented certifications, prices, or real customer
  names/quotes; illustrative content is labeled as such.
- **Isolation:** all marketing artifacts live in `marketing/` (and the corporate
  site in `website/`); they reference but never modify the AdOS application, its
  packages, or its tests.
- Localization QA: a native Turkish reviewer signs off TR copy; a native English
  reviewer signs off EN copy; parity of claims verified.
