## 4. Known limitations

- **Blog:** 20 of the 100 planned articles are written; **80 remain** per
  `BLOG_STRATEGY.md`.
- **LinkedIn:** 156 posts cover one year; a refresh is needed for year two.
- **Assets:** `MARKETING_ASSETS.md` is a **catalog/spec** — the 89 creative files
  are not yet produced (design production is a follow-up).
- **Press Kit:** founder story, executive bios, milestone dates, and awards are
  **placeholders** — no real people, dates, or awards are claimed.
- **Pricing:** value-based; **no specific prices** are published (scoped per
  deployment / Deal Desk).
- **Case/customer content:** any customer example is **explicitly illustrative**;
  no real customers or certifications are claimed.
- **Performance:** local-inference latency (seconds, not milliseconds) is stated
  honestly, not hidden.
- **Product-identity divergence (strategic):** the core application and some
  legacy docs still describe AdOS with **advertising** product copy, while the
  go-to-market materials describe an **Enterprise AI Operating System**. The
  category term is now unified; the deeper reconciliation is a pending decision
  (see `BRAND_AUDIT.md` §4).
